What is Quizabled?

A platform where ability takes the spotlight.

Conceptualized by Seva-in-Action, Quizabled creates engaging opportunities for children and youth with disabilities to learn, compete, collaborate and excel. It brings knowledge beyond textbooks and gives learners space to demonstrate what they know and what they can do.

At its heart is a simple belief: when learning opportunities are accessible and expectations are high, every learner deserves the chance to participate and be recognized.
