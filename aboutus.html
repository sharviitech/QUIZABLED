A journey shaped by learning and participation

Quizabled began in Bengaluru in 2016 with 100 learners with disabilities. The initiative grew from a simple belief: children and youth with disabilities deserve meaningful opportunities to learn, demonstrate their knowledge and celebrate their achievements.

Through accessible quiz experiences, school participation and community collaboration, Quizabled encourages learners to explore new topics, work together and discover what they can do.

Quizabled brings learning to life through curiosity, participation for every learner and opportunities for learners to demonstrate their abilities.

Learning through curiosity

Engaging questions and real-world topics encourage learners to explore ideas, build knowledge and discover new interests.

↗

Participation for every learner

Accessible formats and thoughtful support help learners with different disabilities take part and contribute meaningfully.

◇

Confidence and possibility

Taking part, collaborating and celebrating progress gives learners opportunities to share their strengths and challenge assumptions.

Taking accessible learning further.

Across communities and learning settings, Quizabled creates opportunities to participate, connect and make abilities visible.

⌖

Reaching communities beyond major cities

Quizabled has supported participation from rural, tribal and aspirational districts, including communities where internet connectivity and access to digital devices can be challenging.

Celebrating learners on DD Chandana

State finals for learners with visual and hearing impairments were broadcast in 2024–25. In 2025–26, state finals for learners with intellectual disabilities, cerebral palsy, autism and hearing impairment were also held and broadcast.

✦

Building connections in Assam and Meghalaya

State finals in Guwahati and Shillong helped raise awareness, build local connections and celebrate the abilities of children and youth with disabilities.

◎

Learning through innovation

Through the ILAP pilot, learners with intellectual disabilities and hearing impairments worked in teams, developed ideas and presented projects with support from mentor teachers.

Quizabled and the Sustainable Development Goals

Quizabled supports the global goals of improving access to quality education and reducing inequalities by creating opportunities for differently-abled participants to learn, demonstrate their knowledge and be recognised for their abilities.

Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 4 – Quality Education

Encouraging meaningful learning opportunities and helping learners build knowledge, confidence and skills through engaging quiz experiences.

Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 10 – Reduced Inequalities

Creating opportunities for differently-abled participants to take part, share their strengths and be recognised alongside their peers.

01

Equitable Opportunities

Ensures equal chances for differently-abled participants to demonstrate their knowledge and skills.

02

Challenging Stereotypes

Showcases diverse talents, challenges stereotypes and promotes equality.

03

Empowerment

Empowers differently-abled individuals by recognising and celebrating their abilities.
