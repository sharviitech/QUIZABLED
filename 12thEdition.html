How 12th Edition Works

Through an accessible hybrid format, Quizabled creates opportunities to learn, participate, compete and shine.

01
Step 01 · Online · Nov 2026 onwards

Preliminary Rounds & Semi Finals

Online preliminary rounds across 8 states including rural, tribal, hilly, and remote areas.

02
Step 02 · Offline · Selected states

Zonal Finals

Top performers compete offline on a common platform to win goodies and cash prizes.

03
Step 03 · Offline · March 2027

National Final

A grand national culmination celebrating knowledge, confidence, talent, and inclusion.
